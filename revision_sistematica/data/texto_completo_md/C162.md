The education sector received significant benefits from ChatGPT as an Artificial Intelligence (AI) chatbot because
it  strengthens  human-machine  teamwork  while  making  education  more  efficient  and  adapting  to  individual
students and reducing their workload. This research paper focuses on investigating three primary research ob-
jectives  concerning  the  integration of  ChatGPT into  educational settings.  Firstly,  the  study aims  to  assess  the
effects of ChatGPT on students’ learning outcomes with identifying the main cause and effect impacts. Secondly,
it  seeks  to  identify  and  analyze  various  strategies  that  can  be  employed  to  effectively  utilize  ChatGPT  for
educational purposes. Lastly, the research endeavors to establish the priority levels of these strategies to optimize
the effective implementation of ChatGPT in educational contexts. To achieve these goals, a hybrid multi-criteria
decision-modeling (MCDM) approach, including Decision Making Trial and Evaluation Laboratory (DEMATEL)
and newly developed Comprehensive Distance Based Ranking (COBRA) methods, have been integrated. Using
data  gathered from student questionnaire responses, DEMATEL method identified the main factors impacting
student  behavior  and  academic  practices  by  classifying  twelve  effects  into  cause-and-effect  categories.  This
categorization ensures successful ChatGPT integration in education by assisting policymakers and academics in
creating targeted approaches to address the root cause rather than surface-level  effects. In a focus group dis-
cussion and MCDM analysis based on academicians’ survey responses, the strategy that minimizes AI dependence
that improves critical thinking and promotes self-directed learning was identified as ‘Customize, timely and area
specific assignments’. In order to validate the outcomes derived from the proposed model, alternative MCDM
methodologies,  including  COmplex  PRsitional  ASsessment  (COPRAS),  Combined  Compromise  Solution
(CoCoSo),  Multi-Attributive  Border  Approximation  Area  Comparison  (MABAC),  and  Measurement  of  Alterna-
tives and Ranking According to the COmpromise Solution (MARCOS), have been employed. Subsequently, to
assess the robustness of the proposed model, sensitivity analysis has been conducted under various experimental
conditions. After rigorous evaluation, the COBRA technique emerged as the most appropriate among the models
considered, exhibiting comprehensive calculations and demonstrating the highest Spearman’s correlation with
other models.

1. Introduction

AI has significantly transformed the way different industry’s function
including healthcare as well as finance and manufacturing and educa-
tion  through  better  decision  support  and  automation  and  operational
effectiveness (Zawacki-Richter et al., 2019). Rapid Artificial Intelligence
development now transforms educational systems by delivering students
tutorial  systems  with  artificial  intelligence  together  with  automated
grading and interactive educational content (Luckin and Holmes, 2016).
According to (Qing Ma et al., 2022) ChatGPT stands out as one of the

leading  conversational  AI  models  dedicated  to  helping  students  with
their  academic  assignments  and  concept  understanding  and  research
directions.

In  contrast  to  other  educational  applications  of  AI  like  intelligent
tutoring systems (ITS) and adaptive learning platforms, ChatGPT pro-
vides  a  context-aware,  natural  language  interface  that  can  stimulate
students’  interest  and  deepen  their  understanding  (Zhai,  2022).
Although  AI-enhanced  platforms  like  Coursera’s  AI  tutors,  Khan  Aca-
demy’s adaptive learning systems, and IBM Watson Tutor are based on
structured  knowledge  creation  (Roll  &  Wylie,  2016),  ChatGPT  brings

capability  to  anticipate  lines  of  code  in  different  programming  lan-
guages (Susnjak, 2022). The tool acts as a crucial resource for software
developers because it helps them find code issues and solve problems
promptly  through  instant  identification  of  vulnerabilities  and  errors
(Dwivedi  et  al.,  2023).  With  its  structured  code  teaching  method
ChatGPT  helps  beginners  to  develop  complete  working  software  pro-
grams at a reduced effort level (Kasneci et al., 2023). ChatGPT operates
at  different  levels  of  writing  through  its  premium  edition  to  produce
essays  and  research  papers  and  blogs.  According  to  Zhai  (2022).
Through its framework users achieve better idea organization as well as
enhanced  writing  quality  and  boosted  work  efficiency.  ChatGPT  pro-
vides educational support through features that evaluate assignments as
well  as  review  homework  assignments  and  design  customized  course
syllabi  for  various  academic  standards  (Baidoo-Anu  &  Ansah,  2023).
The  personalized  examination  preparation  tool  for  students  uses
ChatGPT as their primary study planner. The tool produces an intensive
scheduled study plan to help students manage their challenging cour-
sework  effectively  by  processing  information  inputs  related  to  their
available  study  time  and  goal  subjects  and  preferred  study  duration
(Kasneci  et  al.,  2023).  The  AI  system  tailors  its  approach  to  personal
learning styles which leads to better time organization and concentrated
review activities (Baidoo-Anu & Ansah, 2023).

The accessibility benefits that AI-powered tools like  ChatGPT pro-
vide  to  knowledge  yet  they  present  detrimental  effects  to  student
learning practice which requires attention. The academic utilization of
ChatGPT  technology  has  triggered  widespread  concerns  about  dimin-
ished  critical  thinking  abilities  together  with  academic  fraud  and
diminished mental attention and excessive reliance on AI content gen-
eration (Bai et al., 2023). The educational use of ChatGPT faces strong
criticism  because  it  has  the  potential  to  interfere  with  deep  learning
processes along with critical thinking development. Excessive utilization
of AI-generated solutions by students leads to impaired problem-solving
abilities because they primarily receive information instead of engaging
in  analytical  thinking according  to  the research  findings  presented in
Kasneci et al. (2023).

The major academic challenge using ChatGPT stems from students
who  use  the  system  to  create  homework  content  because  they  never
truly  grasp  the  information  (Cotton  et  al.,  2023).  Educational  in-
stitutions  started  doubting  students’  original  work  authenticity  after
implementing  AI  in  assessments  which  created  an  ethical  crisis  and
required  advanced  plagiarism  detection  equipment.  The  utility  of
AI-generated content lacks accurate or unbiased details which students
who use it without analysis may unintentionally process incorrect in-
formation thus affecting their education (van Dis et al., 2023). Through
its design ChatGPT enables users to develop technological dependence
which  impairs  their  ability  to  investigate  independently  and  extract
valuable information from multiple knowledge sources. The practice of
using  AI  as  an  easy  solution  sets  back  the  development  of  student
resilience because students then avoid hard problems that lead to deep

learning (Susnjak, 2022).

A decision-making approach guides this research paper’s systematic
evaluation of how ChatGPT directly affects students with cause and ef-
fect consequences. This research explores both impact types made by
ChatGPT  on  students  before  suggesting  methods  for  controlling  its
adverse  outcomes  and  strengthening  its  beneficial  aspects  for  educa-
tional environments. This analysis presents recommended solutions to
help  educational  personnel  together  with  policy  makers  and  students
handle the implementation of artificial intelligence tools which support
responsible learning.

2. Literature review

2.1. Literature review related to ChatGPT

To  investigate  the  current  available  literature  on  the  influence  on
ChatGPT,  a  thorough  literature  study  was  carried  out.  Only  journal
papers published from 2023 to 2024 are included in the search.

In a study exploring ChatGPT’s potential as an educational tool, its
efficacy in communication, business writing, and composition courses
was examined. After thirty tests, it was discovered that ChatGPT offers
instructors and students enrolled in composition, corporate writing, and
interpersonal  communication  classes  accurate  and  trustworthy  feed-
back.  But  unethical  usage  might  result  in  human  illiteracy  and
unlearning, which would make it more difficult for teachers to distin-
guish apart pupils who are meticulous from those who are dependent on
automation and to assess student progress. The study also revealed that
ChatGPT expertly paraphrases replies that have been created, necessi-
tating software vendors to update their programs in order to keep up
with advances in technology (Alafnan et al., 2023).

In  a  recent  article  the  authors  explore  the  potential  impact  of
ChatGPT on educational practices. Despite uncertainties surrounding its
influence, the study suggests that ChatGPT possesses the capability to
reshape  various  aspects  of  education,  including  learning  objectives,
activities, and evaluation procedures. According to a preliminary study,
ChatGPT enables researchers with little or no specialized writing expe-
rience to produce articles that are methodical, accurate, cohesive, and
correct. The report makes recommendations for changing learning ob-
jectives, emphasizing creativity and critical thinking, and developing AI-
involved  exercises  to  engage  students  in  actual  issues.  There  are  also
worries about outsourcing assessment activities, leading to the sugges-
tion of new designs for tests that place a stronger emphasis on creativity
and critical thinking (Zhai, 2023).

Another recent article investigates the advantages and limitations of
utilizing natural language processing (NLP) models in higher education,
including  ChatGPT  and  Google  Bard.  The  discussion  centers  on
exploring  the  potential  opportunities  and  challenges  associated  with
integrating  NLP  tools  within  educational  contexts.  However,  it  also
notes  the  possible  loss  of  human  connection,  prejudice,  and  ethical
ramifications.  It  emphasizes  the  potential  advantages  of  tailored
learning  and  on-demand  help.  Universities  should  set  rules,  include
students in the creation process, spend money on faculty training, offer
resources and assistance for efficient usage, and employ NLP models as
an alternative to human contact. By delivering customized lessons and
automating administrative work, NLP models can enhance the quality of
education,  but  institutions  must  solve  these  issues  to  guarantee  an
excellent educational experience for their students (Fuchs, 2023).

2.2. Literature review for MCDM models application

As  multiple  studies  present  methods  to  enhance  ChatGPT  benefits
and minimize problems but none establishes an effective priority system
regarding practicality, the evaluation requires implementation of Multi-
Criteria Decision-Modeling (MCDM) methodology. The logical structure
of MCDM analyzes the main causes and effects that shape student in-
teractions with ChatGPT through evaluation and ranking which leads to
decision  outputs  based  on  data  assessment.  The  implementation  of
MCDM will help create an organized framework which explores both
underlying causes of ChatGPT influence along with ranking solutions for
its ethical educational integration.

In today’s world, experts often face difficult choices because there
are so many options, each with its own pros and cons. Traditional ways
of making decisions do not always work well in these complicated sit-
uations. That is why MCDM modeling comes in. It is a way of organizing
information  and  making  decisions  in  a  smart  and  structured  manner.
MCDM  modeling  is  useful  in  many  areas  like  engineering,  business,
finance, the environment, healthcare, and social sciences. There are lots
of different techniques within MCDM that experts have come up with to
help with decision-making. This study looks at some of these techniques
and how they are used in different fields. The full forms of the various
abbreviations are presented in Appendix A with important information to
help readers understand better. The under mentioned following Table 3
illustrates the utilization of different MCDM approaches in addressing a
variety of decision problems within real-world scenarios.

Piegat (2015) utilized TOPSIS, AHP, and COMET models in order to
tackle the chronic liver disease issue. These models aided in evaluating
and prioritizing treatment choices using various health-related factors,
offering  a  structured  way to  choose the  best  treatment course.  In the
selection  of  Enterprise  Resource  Planning  (ERP)  packages,  Ghosh  &
Biswas  (2016)  employed  TOPSIS,  DEMATEL,  and  MACBETH.  These
models  helped  assess  ERP  systems  by  taking  into  account  different
organizational  needs  and  connections  between  criteria,  ultimately
assisting in choosing the best ERP solution. To select cloud render farm
services Ruby Annette et al. (2016) applied AHP and SAW models. These
MCDM models helped in evaluating service providers based on perfor-
mance, cost, and other relevant factors, thus enabling users to choose the
best service for their needs. In a different study, Ceballos et al. (2016)
analyzed  the  application  of  Multi-MOORA,  TOPSIS,  and  VIKOR  for
addressing varied issues which showed how these models can adapt to
different  decision-making  situations,  by  assessing  their  success  in
different settings. CODAS, EDAS, WASPAS, and MOORA were employed
for  assessing  material  handling  equipment  to  help  in  prioritizing
equipment choices according to factors like cost, efficiency, and dura-

evaluating risks associated with PPP projects. Antoniou and Aretoulis
(2018) choose  contracts  for  highway  building  using  MAUT,  TOPSIS,

4. Methods and materials

This  research  explores  the  impacts  identified  from  a  systematic
literature review by employing the DEMATEL method. As suggested by
Li  and  Mathiyazhagan  (2016),  DEMATEL  proves  to  be  the  optimal
approach  for  scrutinizing  interdependencies  among  various  elements
within a complex system. In this investigation, the method is utilized to
attribute  weights  to  the  identified  impacts  and  establish  their  causal
connections. The analytical framework involves a series of mathematical
procedures integral to the DEMATEL methodology.

Steps in DEMATEL:

Step 1: The latest study on ChatGPT’s effects on students’ learning
provided  the  list  of  positive  and  negative  impacts  of  ChatGPT  on
students’ learning.
Step 2: The potential impacts from the impact list that might affect
students  is  sorted  down.  Students’  surveys  served  as  the  basis  for
sorting.  The  research  participants  consist  of  a  diverse  group  who
study  disparate  subjects  and  possess  different  educations  levels  as
well  as  diverse  skills  regarding  AI  tools  allowing  wide-ranging
assessment of ChatGPT’s impact.
Step  3:  To  obtain  an  impact  vs  impact  direct  relationship  matrix
from the same type of respondent in step 2, a survey form ought to be
used.
Step  4:  From  the  many  responses  from  step  3,  a  single  matrix  is
generated.
Step 5: DEMATEL is selected as the technique of choice for a multi-
criteria decision-making process that assesses the importance of the
impacts  with  the  determination  of  the  cause  criteria  and  effect
criteria. Since DEMATEL makes it simple to compute the weight of
the impact from the impact vs impact direct relationship matrix.
Step  6:  The  impacts  are  distinguished  based  on  cause-and-effect
impacts  using  the  DEMATEL  approach.  It  also  allows  for  the  im-
pacts to be ranked.
Step 7: To gather a few suggestions on how to mitigate the negative
effects  of  ChatGPT,  professionals  in  academia  are  engaged  in  a
brainstorming session.
Step 8: The decision matrix, additionally recognized as the impact
vs.  strategy  matrix,  is  produced  through  further  survey  research
among  the  professionals  in  academia.  The  strategies  are  further
evaluated from the Decision matrix according to the strategies based
on the impacts.
Step 9: A single matrix is created from each of the multiple decision
matrices structured from step 8.
Step 10: Strategies are ranked by COBRA method.
Step 11: Sensitivity analysis is carried out for the proposed model
(DEMATEL & COBRA), and the proposed model is compared with the
other three models.
Step 12: The best strategy is ranked for effective teaching method for
mitigating the negative impact and enhance the positive impact.

The methodology facilitates the research study to first comprehend
how  ChatGPT  affects  learning  (RQ-1),  then  create  practical  strategies
based  on  the  impacts  (RQ-2),  and  lastly  rank  the  strategies  for  best
execution (RQ-3).

8. Discussion and conclusion

Unlike  previous  works,  which  has  primarily  explored  qualitative
assessments  without  distinguishing  the  impacts  between  causing  and
being caused, this study offers a pedagogical data analytics framework
based on three phases was used to fully evaluate the educational impact
that  ChatGPT  has  on  the  learning  behavior  and  performance  among
students considering the learners and academicians as the stakeholder
groups. This combination of methods enabled the given study not only to
examine the perceived effects of ChatGPT but also to determine what
strategic measures could be introduced to maximize its advantages and
minimize its possible shortcomings in the educational context. In order
to  achieve  the  classification,  extensive  literature  reviews  were  con-
ducted, and as was previously mentioned in phase 1 in research meth-
odology,  twelve  significant  effects  of  ChatGPT  were  ultimately
determined by this study using a strong methodological framework. The
second  phase  which  was  subsequently  separated  the  effects  into  two
categories: cause and effect, involved the formulation of a comprehen-
sive questionnaire that collected the perceptions of ChatGPT effects on
students in the academic experience. Based on our analysis from the data
collected  from  the  impact  vs  impact  matrix  on  the  questionnaire,  we
found out that the reduce effort, save time, learning without a teacher,
unlearning, lack of creativity and escape similarity check by turnitin are
the major cause impact on students, those factors that directly influence
the  behavior  and  academic  practices  of  the  students.  The  remaining
impacts  identified  are  of  the  effect  impacts  which  are  the  following
consequences  of  the  mentioned  causes.  This  structured  classification
provides academicians, and policymakers with an ability to understand
the  underlying  mechanisms  of  ChatGPT’s  influence  and  develop  and
implement  targeted  strategies  to  tackle  those  mechanisms,  namely
resolving the root causes and not the surface level effect.

A  number  of educational  innovations are presented  in the  current
study, which makes it unique among other sources. To begin with, it is
the first to introduce a classification of causes and effects to assess the

pedagogical effects of ChatGPT, which is hardly observed in previous
literature. This well-organized classification allows stakeholders to focus
on underlying causes of student behavior, as opposed to consequences
on  the  surface.  Second,  the  MCDM  model  used  (a  hybrid  DEMATEL-
COBRA) considers simultaneously both the learner and educator point
of  views  and  can  be  characterized  as  a  multi-stakeholder,  data-based
approach to pedagogical-decision making. Third, the study advocates an
educational approach that has not been discussed previously in regard to
self-directed  learning,  contextualized,  area-specific  assignments  that
enabled the students to interest themselves with real-world content to
avoid high rates of information dependence on AI. Collectively, these
innovations can be said to do not only more responsible integration of AI
in education, but also to lead to the development of evidence-based and
pedagogically aligned learning design in the era of artificial intelligence.
In  addition  to  the  relevance  of  methodological  innovation,  the
research grants significant pedagogical contributions since it bases the
results  on  proven  theories  of  education.  In  the  analysis,  one  can  find
many similarities with the constructivist learning theory that focuses on
active and contextualized learning based on problem solving by moving
in  the  real  world.  The  suggested  approach  of  creating  custom,  time-
sensitive, and location-based tasks does not just serve as the deterrent
of the passive use of AI tools but also leads to more critical thinking,
which  is  central  to  constructivism.  The  way  ChatGPT  has  been  suc-
cessfully implemented as a scaffold and used as an incremental assis-
tance  tool  and  not  as  a  source  of  information  is  also  aligned  with
Vygotsky Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) and thus, AI can be used
as an aid in learning as long as there is expertise in guiding the tool (Saul,
2024). Such theoretical mergers depict that the use of the tools based on
AI, when embedded pedagogically can be harnessed beyond solutions to
automation  or  the  delivery  of  content,  but  also  as  sources  of  greater
learning, critical thinking, and pedagogical innovation. This study can
also  contribute  to  filling  the  gap  between  an  AI  implementation  and
pedagogy effectiveness because the presented framework can be applied
to the dynamic discussion of AI-enhanced schooling.

limitations.  Firstly,  since  the  direct  matrix  and  pairwise  comparisons
involve  student  responses,  there  is  a  chance  that  the  findings  will  be
skewed.  Second,  the  study  only  included  data  from  Bangladeshi

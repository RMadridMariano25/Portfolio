This research examines the use of ChatGPT among university-level students in the United Arab Emirates (UAE)
and  its  effects  on  their  learning  experiences.  The  precise  focus  remains  on  the  effects  of  ChatGPT  usage  on
Student Engagement, Critical Thinking Abilities, and Academic Achievement. Using the cross-sectional design,
the Constructivism Learning Theory supports this research. Data gathered using 353 structured questionnaires is
analyzed  using  Partial  Least  Square-Structural  Equation  Modelling  (PLS-SEM).  Results  showed  that  ChatGPT
usage  positively  affects  student  engagement  in  the  learning  process.  The  effect  of  ChatGPT  usage  on  Critical
Thinking Abilities also remained significant. Finally, the findings indicated the positive effect of ChatGPT usage
on the Academic Achievement of Emirati students. These results imply a robust, positive, and constructive role of
AI technology, particularly ChatGPT, in the education and learning journey of university students in the UAE. It
is concluded that ChatGPT is a useful tool that helps students by providing resources and suggestions throughout
their  learning  process.  It  increases  engagement,  effort,  and  ambition  in  academic  tasks,  enhancing  academic
achievement. ChatGPT supports educational progress and motivates students to obtain knowledge by improving
their interest in learning. Finally, the study’s implications and limitations are discussed. Also, recommendations
for future studies are proposed.

2.2. ChatGPT and students’ engagement

Student  engagement  is  the  extent  to  which  students  are  actively
involved  and  ingrained  in  their  learning  experiences.  It  contains
different  dimensions,  including  behavioral,  emotional,  and  cognitive
engagement. Engaged students partake attentively in classroom activ-
ities, show a readiness to learn, and positively interact with peers and
educators. This engagement  is mirrored in students’  confidence, curi-
osity, enjoyment of learning tasks, and sense of belonging and associa-
tion with the learning community (Ali et al., 2023; Firat, 2023; Hamid
et  al.,  2023).  Concerning  ChatGPT  and  students’  engagement,  the
generative  AI  models  have  intrigued  public  interest  because  of  their
unique  ability  to  produce  content  nearly  resembling  human-created
material.  ChatGPT  has  especially  shown  exceptional  performance
across  different  domains  of  application.  It  excels  in  tasks  such  as
generating readable content and essays, working as a chatbot, decoding
languages, answering questions, and assisting in programming code and
existing literature. It shows that ChatGPT provides a range of abilities
beyond helping students with assessment design, essay generation, and

3

language translation. It enables users to ask and answer myriad ques-
tions, outline texts, converse with them, and interact with peers (Sok,
2023). According to Baidoo-Anu and Ansah (2023), this model exhibits
creative writing abilities across diverse topics, from brief paragraphs to
complete  research  articles,  which  can  be  highly  convincing  and
appealing  to  the  students.  Atlas  (2023) also  supports  combining
ChatGPT and similar language models in higher education, citing their
possible  applications  in  writing  support,  language  learning,  research
initiatives, and administrative chores. Adiguzel et al. (2023) also stated
that  ChatGPT  presents  multiple  prospects  for  learners,  i.e.,  increased
intrinsic motivation, enabling a deeper understanding of concepts, and
promoting expertise development. The automation enabled by ChatGPT
is  critical  in  expediting  lesson  design,  allowing  educators  to  create
engaging and effective learning materials more efficiently.

2.3. ChatGPT and students’ critical thinking abilities

Students’ critical thinking abilities are their approach to systemati-
cally analyze, assess, and synthesize information. It involves applying
higher-order  cognitive  skills,  including  logical  reasoning,  problem-
solving,  decision-making,  and  metacognition,  to  discern  the  validity,
applicability,  and  importance  of  information  and  arguments  (Barta
et al., 2022; Zhou, 2018). In this regard, the versatility of ChatGPT in
higher education has acquired attention as a compelling area due to its
myriad applications (Essel et al., 2024). Several researchers (Mollick &
Mollick,  2022;  Tlili  et  al.,  2023)  have  even  supported  incorporating
ChatGPT  into  instructional  practices  to  improve  interactive  learning
experiences. Also, it guarantees students’ cognitive skills development
(Exintaris et al., 2023). Also, there is an advancement in the develop-
ment  of  assessment  techniques,  with  many  educational  institutions
transitioning  towards  verbal  examinations,  combined  projects,  and
handwritten evaluations (Guo &  Lee, 2023; Shanto et al., 2024). This
shift  in  assessment  methods  highlights  the  importance  of  assessing
prompts  and  analyzing  the  synthesized  outcomes,  facilitating  knowl-
edge  creation,  and  improving  the  relationship  between  creative  and
critical thinking (Adiguzel et al., 2023). In their study, Essel et al. (2024)
examined the possible benefits of using ChatGPT to improve students’
cognitive  skills.  The  study  showed  that  incorporating  ChatGPT  into
classroom activities can positively affect students’ critical, creative, and
meditative thinking abilities. Notably, the group that received help from
ChatGPT showed substantial improvements in these skills compared to
the control group, indicating that ChatGPT can effectively contribute to
developing these cognitive abilities.

2.5. Research objectives and research questions

Considering  the  role  and  importance  of  ChatGPT  for  students  in
higher education, this research aims to examine the effect of ChatGPT
usage on student learning, including their engagement in educational
activities, critical thinking abilities, and academic attainment. Despite
the  applicability  of  constructivist  theory  in  academic  research,  there
remains a theoretical gap in comprehending how ChatGPT aligns with
the principles of constructivism and affects students’ learning processes.
While  Constructivist  Theory  highlights  the  active  construction  of
knowledge  through  social  interactions  and  means  of  experiences,  its
application to the incorporation of ChatGPT in educational settings has
yet  to  be  investigated.  Thus,  this  research  addresses  three  research
questions: (i) How does ChatGPT affect students’ engagement? (ii) How
does  ChatGPT  affect  critical  thinking  abilities?  and  (iii)  How  does
ChatGPT affect students’ academic achievement?

2.6. Significance of the study

This research contributes to the wider debate on the role of artificial
intelligence  in  education.  As  AI  technologies  like  ChatGPT  become
increasingly  prevailing,  evaluating  their  possible  benefits  and  re-
strictions in sustaining student learning is critical. This study addresses
this need by examining the multifaceted effects of ChatGPT on student
engagement,  critical  thinking,  and  academic  achievement.  These  in-
sights  are  essential  for  policymakers  and  educational  stakeholders  to
harness AI’s transformative potential responsibly.

3. Methodology

This  research  involves  a  cross-sectional  design,  which  provides
generalizable  results  within  a  precise  period  (Abbott  &  McKinney,
2013). This study was conducted from January 2024 to April 2024. The
researchers  gathered  data  by  employing  structured  questionnaires
designed on a five-point Likert scale, as suggested by Aizpurua (2020).
The respondents accessed the directory after obtaining permission from
their  respective  universities.  Notably,  informed  consent  was  obtained
from all the respondents, who were informed about the primary research
ethics. They were also told that their data would be used only for this
research and that the researchers would refrain from using their data for
commercial purposes. Once the data was obtained, they were evaluated
and  coded  for  analysis.  The  data  analysis  involved  descriptive  and
inferential  statistics  through  SPSS  and  partial  least  square-structural
equation modeling.

3.1. Sampling approaches

The current research population involves university-level students in
the  United  Arab  Emirates.  However,  per  the  former  research  re-
quirements, the population was further narrowed into a subset or sample
to meet the basic research criteria. Notably, with a total population of
6540 students in two public sector universities in Ajman (4541 + 1999)
(Ajman University, 2024; City University Ajman, 2023), the researchers
used  Yamane’s  formula  of  sample  size  calculation,  a  sample  of  376
students  was  initially selected (Adam,  2020). For data  gathering pur-
poses,  the  researchers  personally  visited  the  selected  institutions  and
distributed questionnaires among the study respondents. The question-
naires were distributed among the respondents, and the data gathered
occurred from January 13, 2024, to March 5, 2024. It is worth noting
that the questionnaires were randomly distributed without any further
criteria  based  on  age,  major,  gender,  and  others.  Once  the  data  was
gathered,  the  acquired  questionnaires  were  carefully  evaluated.  23
questionnaires were empty or incorrectly filled in by the respondents.
Therefore,  353  questionnaires  were  finalized  for  the  current  study,

nature of ChatGPT makes it a useful tool for creating more engaging and
immersive  learning  experiences.  This  aligns  with  the  constructivist
perspective that highlights the significance of active participation in the
learning process. Using ChatGPT, educators can create a more interac-
tive and supportive learning environment that encourages students and
improves  their  confidence  in  conducting  research  and  completing  as-
signments.  The  enhancement  of  critical  thinking  abilities  is  another
significant implication. The study suggests that students usually ques-
tion the precision and reliability of information provided by ChatGPT,
prompting  them  to  seek  further  clarification  and  evidence.  This  vali-
dation and quest for alternative viewpoints align with the constructivist
approach, encouraging learners to critically evaluate information and
develop a deeper understanding through inquiry and reflection. There-
fore, incorporating ChatGPT into the curriculum can stimulate critical
thinking skills as students learn to analyze and corroborate information
independently.

Furthermore,  the  research  implies  that  ChatGPT  contributes  posi-
tively to academic achievement. Students reported that ChatGPT brings
innovative ideas, provides robust writing guidance, and improves their
overall academic performance and learning pace. This improvement can
be attributed to the constructivist principle that knowledge is built upon
prior understanding and experiences. ChatGPT helps students construct
new knowledge more effectively and efficiently by offering personalized
assistance and new perspectives. Therefore, the practical implications of
this  research  are  clear:  ChatGPT  is  a  powerful  educational  tool  that
improves  student  engagement,  critical  thinking,  and  academic

performance. Educators in higher education, especially in Ajman, UAE,
should consider incorporating ChatGPT into their teaching practices to
create a more engaged and supportive learning environment. By doing
so, they can benefit from this advanced writing model to support stu-
dents’ educational journeys and promote a more active, meditative, and
successful learning experience.

6. Conclusion

This  research  investigated  the  effect  of  ChatGPT  on  students’
learning experiences, specifically their engagement in education, critical
thinking abilities, and academic achievement in two public sector uni-
versities  in  Ajman,  UAE.  Results  provided  robust  insights  for  the
educational  sector  by  analyzing  the  effect  of  ChatGPT  technology  on
student learning results. By examining aspects like engagement, critical
thinking,  and  academic  achievement,  the  study  suggests  practical  in-
sights into the effectiveness of incorporating AI technology into educa-
tional activities. These discernments are essential for educators aiming
for  innovative  approaches  to  improve  student  learning  experiences.
Also, understanding the effect of ChatGPT informs the development of
teaching  strategies  that  employ  technology  to  meet  students’  myriad
learning needs. Also, the results of this study have practical implications
for educational policy and technology development. Policymakers can
use the acquired insights to develop guidelines assuring AI technologies’
ethical  and  effective  integration  in  academic  settings.  Finally,  this
research magnifies evidence-based decision-making in education, pro-
moting a more in-depth understanding of how technology can improve
student  learning  experiences  in  the  best  possible  manner.  Therefore,
ChatGPT is a useful tool that nurtures student curiosity and interest and
assists  them  throughout  their  learning  process  by  providing  practical
resources,  guidance,  and  interactive  features.  Students  become  more
deeply engaged, invest greater effort, and approach their academic tasks
with  advanced  initiative.  ChatGPT  contributes  positively  to  academic
achievement by encouraging students’ interest in learning. When used
effectively,  ChatGPT  plays  a  critical  role  in  promoting  educational
progress  and  attaining  better  results  in  students’  academic  efforts,
therefore maintaining their motivation to acquire knowledge.

Limitations and recommendations for future research

While  this  study  offers  practical  insight  regarding  ChatGPT  usage
among  students  according  to  their  perspectives,  it  has  some  primary
limitations.  First,  this  research  was  conducted  specifically  in  Ajman,
UAE,  which  limits  the  generalizability  of  results.  The  findings  of  this
research are based on data collected from two public sector universities,
which may limit the generalizability of the results to the broader context
of  the  UAE.  These  universities’  specific  cultural,  educational,  and
institutional factors may differ from those in private institutions or other
regions. Additionally, public universities in the UAE may have unique
characteristics that do not fully represent the diversity of educational
environments across the country. Therefore, caution should be exercised
when attempting to generalize these findings to the entire UAE higher
